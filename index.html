About

Welcome to my homepage! I am currently a PhD candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Electrical Engineering at West Virginia University, supervised by Dr. Xin Li. My research spans deep learning, computer vision, and object detection, with a growing focus on applications in environmental sustainability and biometric identification.
